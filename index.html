Major Festivals

Diwali: The festival of lights, celebrating the victory of light over darkness.

Holi: The festival of colors, marking the arrival of spring and joy.

Navratri: A nine-night celebration dedicated to dance, devotion, and culture.

Cultural Heritage

India has a rich history filled with classical dance forms, ancient architecture, traditional handlooms, and diverse regional traditions passed down through generations.
